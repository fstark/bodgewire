# Bouncing Ball

## What it does

Connect +5 V to `J1` and a TTL monitor to `J2`. After power-up, the monitor shows a white box with a dashed line down the middle, and a small square ball moving diagonally inside it. The ball bounces off each of the four walls, crosses the box in about a second and never stops. The picture locks within half a second; just after power-up the ball may be out of sight for a moment before it comes in through a wall.
