# 74LS139 — dual 2-to-4 line decoder

## Function

Two separate decoders in one package. In half 1, a 2-bit number on `1B`, `1A` (`1B` the higher bit) picks one of `1Y0_N`–`1Y3_N`, which goes low; the other three stay high. Half 2 does the same with its `2` pins. In a computer each half gives one group of memory chips their selects from two address lines.

Each half works only while its enable (`1G_N`, `2G_N`) is low; high puts all four of its outputs high, whatever its select inputs say.

Delays (TI SN74LS139A, typical): about 13 ns for an output to rise and 22 ns to fall after a select input changes; 16 ns and 21 ns after the enable.

## Pins

- `1A`, `1B`: select inputs of half 1; `1A` is the lower bit
- `1G_N`: enable of half 1, active low
- `1Y0_N`–`1Y3_N`: outputs of half 1, the selected one low
- `2A`, `2B`, `2G_N`, `2Y0_N`–`2Y3_N`: the same for half 2
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
