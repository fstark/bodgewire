# 74LS273 — octal D flip-flop, clear

## Function

Eight flip-flops on one clock: an 8-bit register. On each rising edge of `CLK`, each of `1Q`–`8Q` takes its own input among `1D`–`8D` as it was just before the edge, and holds it until the next rising edge, whatever the input does meanwhile. On a computer's data bus it is an output port: the processor writes a byte, a pulse on `CLK` at the end of the write keeps it, and the outputs drive lamps or other circuits until the next write.

`CLR_N` low clears all eight at once (`1Q`–`8Q` low), without the clock, and holds them clear while it stays low. Wired to the reset line, it starts the port at zero.

## Pins

- `1D`–`8D`: data inputs
- `1Q`–`8Q`: outputs
- `CLK`: clock; `1D`–`8D` are copied on the rising edge
- `CLR_N`: clear, active low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
