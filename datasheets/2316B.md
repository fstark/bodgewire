# 2316B — 2K x 8 mask ROM, mask-set chip selects

## Function

A memory of 2048 bytes, fixed when the chip is made. The byte at the address on `A0`–`A10` appears on `O0`–`O7` about 450 ns after the address settles, while all three chip selects are active. Otherwise the outputs are off (floating), so that several memories can share the same data lines.

Whether each chip select is active high or active low is also chosen when the chip is made. The Part's `select=` says it: one letter for each of `CS1`, `CS2`, `CS3`, `L` for active low and `H` for active high. That is why the pin names carry no `_N`. On the PET 2001:

- `CS1` is active low, on the decoder output for its 4 KB block;
- `CS2` is on `A11`: active low for the lower 2 KB of the block, active high for the upper;
- `CS3` is active high, tied to +5 V.

The outputs come on about 120 ns after the selects become active, and go off about 100 ns after one becomes inactive. These are the data sheet's maximums; no typical values are published.

## Pins

- `A0`–`A9`, `A10`: address; `A0` is the lowest bit
- `O0`–`O7`: data out; `O0` is the lowest bit
- `CS1`, `CS2`, `CS3`: chip selects, each active high or low as the chip was made
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
