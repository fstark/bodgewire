# SLIDE — slide switch

## Function

A single slide switch in a one-position DIP body: its two legs are joined while the knob is at ON, apart while it is at the other end. Unlike a push button or a key, it stays where it is moved, through power off and on, until it is moved back.

## Pins

- `1`, `2`: the two legs, pads 1 and 2, joined while ON
