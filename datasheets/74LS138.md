# 74LS138 — 3-to-8 line decoder

## Function

Turns a 3-bit number into one of eight lines: the output numbered by `C`, `B`, `A` (`C` the highest bit) goes low, the other seven stay high. In a computer it sits on the top address lines and gives each memory or port chip its own block of addresses: each output is one chip's select.

The chip works only while `G1` is high and both `G2A_N` and `G2B_N` are low. Any other level on them puts all eight outputs high, whatever `A`, `B` and `C` say; a second decoder or a clock phase can be wired to them.

## Pins

- `A`, `B`, `C`: select inputs; `A` is the lowest bit
- `Y0_N`–`Y7_N`: outputs, the selected one low
- `G1`: enable, active high
- `G2A_N`, `G2B_N`: enables, active low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
