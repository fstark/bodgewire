# 74LS32 — quad 2-input OR

## Function

Four separate OR gates. Each output is high when either input (or both) is high; it is low only when both inputs are low.

- A low, B low: Y low
- A low, B high: Y high
- A high, B low: Y high
- A high, B high: Y high

## Pins

- `1A`–`4A`, `1B`–`4B`: inputs; gate n uses nA and nB
- `1Y`–`4Y`: outputs
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
