# 2114 — 1K x 4 static RAM

## Function

A memory of 1024 words of 4 bits, which can be read and written while the power is on. Its contents are random at power-on and lost when the power goes off. Two of them side by side make 1024 bytes.

`CS_N` low selects the chip. With `WE_N` high, the word at the address on `A0`–`A9` appears on `IO1`–`IO4` at most 450 ns after the address settles (300 ns for the faster 2114-3, 200 ns for the 2114-2), or 120 ns after `CS_N` falls. With `WE_N` low, the chip writes `IO1`–`IO4` into that word and its outputs are off (floating), so that another chip can drive the same lines; the address and the data must stay put until `WE_N` goes back high, and the word keeps what they held then. With `CS_N` high the outputs are off.

The inputs are MOS: a pin left floating keeps reading its last value.

## Pins

- `A0`–`A9`: address; `A0` is the lowest bit
- `IO1`–`IO4`: data in and out; `IO1` is the lowest bit
- `CS_N`: chip select, active low
- `WE_N`: write enable, active low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
