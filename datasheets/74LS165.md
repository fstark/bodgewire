# 74LS165 — 8-bit parallel-in shift register

## Function

Eight stages in a row, `A` first and `H` last; only the last one, `QH`, comes out (and its inverse `QH_N`). While `SH_LD_N` is low, the eight stages take `A`–`H` at once, without waiting for the clock, and `QH` shows `H`. While it is high, each rising clock edge moves every stage one place towards `QH` (the old `QH` is lost), and the first stage takes `SER`.

The clock is `CLK` OR `CLK_INH`: while `CLK_INH` is high the clock is stopped. Tie it low when unused. Used to turn a byte into a stream of bits, one bit a clock: on a video board, the dots of one line of a character, one dot a clock.

## Pins

- `A`–`H`: parallel inputs, loaded while `SH_LD_N` is low; `H` comes out first
- `SER`: serial input, shifted into the first stage
- `SH_LD_N`: low to load, high to shift
- `CLK`: clock; the register shifts on its rising edge
- `CLK_INH`: clock inhibit, high stops the clock
- `QH`: the last stage
- `QH_N`: the last stage, inverted
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
