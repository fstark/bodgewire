# 74LS107 — dual JK flip-flop, clear

## Function

Two separate flip-flops. Flip-flop 1 changes only when `1CLK` **falls**, from the `1J` and `1K` it had just before the edge:

- `1J` low, `1K` low: `1Q` keeps its value;
- `1J` high, `1K` low: `1Q` goes high (set);
- `1J` low, `1K` high: `1Q` goes low (clear);
- `1J` high, `1K` high: `1Q` changes to the opposite (toggle).

`1CLR_N` low clears it at once, without waiting for a clock, and holds it there. `1Q_N` is always the opposite of `1Q`. There is no preset input. Flip-flop 2 works the same on `2CLK`, `2J`, `2K`, `2CLR_N`, `2Q` and `2Q_N`.

## Pins

- `1J`, `1K`: inputs of flip-flop 1
- `1CLK`: clock of flip-flop 1, falling edge (pin 12)
- `1CLR_N`: clear of flip-flop 1, active low (pin 13)
- `1Q`, `1Q_N`: outputs of flip-flop 1
- `2J`, `2K`: inputs of flip-flop 2
- `2CLK`: clock of flip-flop 2, falling edge
- `2CLR_N`: clear of flip-flop 2, active low
- `2Q`, `2Q_N`: outputs of flip-flop 2
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
