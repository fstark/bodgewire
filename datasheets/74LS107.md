# 74LS107 — dual JK flip-flop, clear

## Function

Two separate flip-flops. Each changes only when its clock **falls**, from the `J` and `K` it had just before the edge:

- `J` low, `K` low: `Q` keeps its value;
- `J` high, `K` low: `Q` goes high (set);
- `J` low, `K` high: `Q` goes low (clear);
- `J` high, `K` high: `Q` changes to the opposite (toggle).

`CLR_N` low clears the flip-flop at once, without waiting for a clock, and holds it there. `Q_N` is always the opposite of `Q`. There is no preset input.

## Pins

- `1J`, `1K`: inputs of flip-flop 1
- `1CLK`: clock of flip-flop 1, falling edge (pin 12)
- `1CLR_N`: clear of flip-flop 1, active low (pin 13)
- `1Q`, `1Q_N`: outputs of flip-flop 1
- `2J`, `2K`: inputs of flip-flop 2
- `2CLK`: clock of flip-flop 2, falling edge
- `2CLR_N`: clear of flip-flop 2, active low
- `2Q`, `2Q_N`: outputs of flip-flop 2
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
