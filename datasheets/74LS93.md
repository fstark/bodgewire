# 74LS93 — 4-bit binary counter

## Function

Two counters in one package, both stepping on the **falling** edge of their clock:

- `CKA` → `QA`: divides by two;
- `CKB` → `QB`–`QD`: divides by eight, counting 0 to 7 on `QB` (lowest) to `QD`.

Wire `QA` to `CKB` outside the chip and feed the clock to `CKA`: the four outputs then count 0 to 15 in binary, `QA` lowest bit. Inside, the ÷8 is a ripple of flip-flops: after a clock edge `QB` changes first and `QD` last, about 34 ns after the edge. `QD` falls when the ÷8 goes from 7 back to 0, so it can clock another counter, or the chip's own `CKA`.

`R01` and `R02` both high clear both counters to 0 without waiting for a clock, and hold them there; while they do, clock edges are ignored.

Unlike most 14-pin chips, the power pins are in the middle of the rows: **`VCC` is pin 5 and `GND` pin 10**. Pins 4, 6, 7 and 13 are not connected.

## Pins

- `CKA`: clock of the ÷2 counter, falling edge
- `CKB`: clock of the ÷8 counter, falling edge
- `QA`: output of the ÷2 counter
- `QB`–`QD`: outputs of the ÷8 counter, `QB` lowest
- `R01`, `R02`: clear to 0 when both are high
- `NC4`, `NC6`, `NC7`, `NC13`: not connected
- `VCC`: +5 V, pin 5
- `GND`: 0 V, pin 10
