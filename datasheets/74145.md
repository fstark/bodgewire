# 74145 — BCD-to-decimal decoder/driver, open collector

## Function

Turns a 4-bit number on `D` `C` `B` `A` into one of ten outputs: the output with that number (0 to 9) is pulled low and the other nine are released. The numbers 10 to 15 release all ten. The outputs are open collector: they only pull down, so each needs something pulling it up (a resistor, a lamp, a key matrix's column resistor through a held key), and they can sink the current of an LED digit's common cathode. Scanning a keypad or a row of digits: count `D`–`A` and each output goes low in turn.

## Pins

- `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`: the number, `A` the lowest bit
- `Y0_N`–`Y9_N`: outputs, open collector, low when selected
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
