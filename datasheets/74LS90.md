# 74LS90 — decade counter

## Function

Two counters in one package, both stepping on the **falling** edge of their clock:

- `CKA` → `QA`: divides by two;
- `CKB` → `QB`–`QD`: divides by five, counting 0 to 4 on `QB` (lowest) to `QD`.

Wire `QA` to `CKB` outside the chip and feed the clock to `CKA`: the four outputs then count 0 to 9 in binary-coded decimal, `QA` lowest bit. `QD` is high for 8 and 9 and falls when the count goes from 9 back to 0, so it clocks the `CKA` of the next decade.

Without waiting for a clock:

- `R91` and `R92` both high: the count is 9;
- otherwise `R01` and `R02` both high: the count is 0;
- otherwise the counter counts.

Unlike most 14-pin chips, the power pins are in the middle of the rows: **`VCC` is pin 5 and `GND` pin 10**. Pins 4 and 13 are not connected.

## Pins

- `CKA`: clock of the ÷2 counter, falling edge
- `CKB`: clock of the ÷5 counter, falling edge
- `QA`: output of the ÷2 counter
- `QB`–`QD`: outputs of the ÷5 counter, `QB` lowest
- `R01`, `R02`: reset to 0 when both are high
- `R91`, `R92`: set to 9 when both are high
- `NC4`, `NC13`: not connected
- `VCC`: +5 V, pin 5
- `GND`: 0 V, pin 10
