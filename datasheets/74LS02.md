# 74LS02 — quad 2-input NOR

## Function

Four separate NOR gates. Each output is high only when both of its inputs are low; otherwise it is low.

- A low, B low: Y high
- A low, B high: Y low
- A high, B low: Y low
- A high, B high: Y low

Unlike most chips here, the outputs come first: pin 1 is output `1Y`, pins 2 and 3 its inputs.

## Pins

- `1A`–`4A`, `1B`–`4B`: inputs; gate n uses nA and nB
- `1Y`–`4Y`: outputs
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
