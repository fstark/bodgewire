# 74LS157 — quad 2-to-1 multiplexer

## Function

Four switches sharing one control: each output `Y` copies its `A` input while `S` is low, and its `B` input while `S` is high. Used to send one of two 4-bit numbers to the same place, for example two counters into one decoder, taking turns.

`G_N` high turns every `Y` off (low), whatever `S` and the data are. Tie it low when unused.

## Pins

- `1A`–`4A`: data inputs, chosen while `S` is low
- `1B`–`4B`: data inputs, chosen while `S` is high
- `1Y`–`4Y`: outputs
- `S`: select, low for `A`, high for `B`
- `G_N`: strobe, active low; high forces every `Y` low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
