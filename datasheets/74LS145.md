# 74LS145 — BCD-to-decimal decoder/driver, open collector

## Function

The low-power Schottky version of the 74145. It turns a 4-bit number on `D` `C` `B` `A` into one of ten outputs: the output with that number (0 to 9) is pulled low and the other nine are released. The numbers 10 to 15 release all ten. The outputs are open collector: they only pull down, so each needs something pulling it up. On the PET 2001 it scans the keyboard: the number on `A`–`D` picks a row, and a held key carries that row's low to its column.

## Pins

- `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`: the number, `A` the lowest bit
- `Y0_N`–`Y9_N`: outputs, open collector, low when selected
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
