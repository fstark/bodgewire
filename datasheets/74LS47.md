# 74LS47 — BCD to 7-segment decoder, open collector

## Function

Shows the number on `A`–`D` (binary-coded decimal, `A` lowest bit) on a common-anode 7-segment digit. Each segment output goes low to light its segment: wire it to the digit's segment pin, with the digit's anode at +5 V through a resistor. The outputs are open collector: they only pull low, and let go otherwise.

Segments are named as on the digit: `a` top, `b` top right, `c` bottom right, `d` bottom, `e` bottom left, `f` top left, `g` middle.

- 0 to 9 are drawn as usual, except that 6 has no top bar (`a`) and 9 no bottom bar (`d`).
- 10 to 14 light odd fragments: 10 `d` `e` `g`, 11 `c` `d` `g`, 12 `b` `f` `g`, 13 `a` `d` `f` `g`, 14 `d` `e` `f` `g`.
- 15 lights nothing.

Control inputs, all active low, in this order of priority:

- `BI_N` low: every segment dark;
- `LT_N` low (lamp test): every segment lit;
- `RBI_N` low: a 0 is shown dark (to blank leading zeros).

On the real chip pin 4 is both the blanking input and the ripple-blanking output that chains leading-zero blanking to the next digit; here it is the input only. Tie `LT_N`, `RBI_N` and `BI_N` high when unused.

## Pins

- `A`–`D`: the number, `A` lowest bit
- `SA_N`–`SG_N`: segment outputs `a` to `g`, open collector, low = lit
- `LT_N`: lamp test, lights every segment when low
- `BI_N`: blanking input, darkens every segment when low
- `RBI_N`: ripple-blanking input, darkens a 0 when low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
