# 74LS164 — 8-bit serial-in shift register

## Function

Eight stages in a row, `QA` first and `QH` last. On each rising edge of `CLK`, every stage takes the value of the one before it (`QA` → `QB`, … `QG` → `QH`; the old `QH` is lost), and `QA` takes `A` AND `B`. Tie one of `A`, `B` high to use the other as the serial input.

`CLR_N` low sets all eight outputs to 0 at once, without waiting for the clock, and holds them there.

## Pins

- `A`, `B`: serial data in; `QA` takes `A` AND `B`
- `CLK`: clock; the register shifts on its rising edge
- `CLR_N`: clear, active low
- `QA`–`QH`: the eight stages, `QA` first
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
