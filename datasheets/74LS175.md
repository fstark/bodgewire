# 74LS175 — quad D flip-flop, clear

## Function

Four flip-flops on one clock, each remembering one bit: a 4-bit register. On each rising edge of `CLK`, each of `1Q`–`4Q` takes its own input among `1D`–`4D` as it was just before the edge, and holds it until the next rising edge, whatever the input does meanwhile. `1Q_N`–`4Q_N` are always the opposites of `1Q`–`4Q`. Often used to hold a number steady while the circuit that made it moves on.

`CLR_N` low clears all four at once (`1Q`–`4Q` low), without the clock, and holds them clear while it stays low. Tie it high when unused.

## Pins

- `1D`–`4D`: data inputs
- `1Q`–`4Q`: outputs
- `1Q_N`–`4Q_N`: inverted outputs
- `CLK`: clock; `1D`–`4D` are copied on the rising edge
- `CLR_N`: clear, active low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
