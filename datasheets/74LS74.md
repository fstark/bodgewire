# 74LS74 — dual D flip-flop, preset and clear

## Function

Two separate flip-flops, each remembering one bit. On the rising edge of its clock, a flip-flop copies its `D` input to `Q`; `Q_N` is always the opposite of `Q`. Between clock edges the output holds, whatever `D` does. Often used to delay a signal by exactly one clock, or (with `Q_N` wired back to `D`) to divide a frequency by two.

Preset and clear act at once, without the clock:

- `PRE_N` low: `Q` high
- `CLR_N` low: `Q` low
- both low: `Q` and `Q_N` both high (not a normal state)

## Pins

- `1D`, `2D`: data inputs
- `1CLK`, `2CLK`: clocks; `D` is copied on the rising edge
- `1Q`, `2Q`: outputs
- `1Q_N`, `2Q_N`: inverted outputs
- `1PRE_N`, `2PRE_N`: preset, active low
- `1CLR_N`, `2CLR_N`: clear, active low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
