# 2332 — 4K x 8 mask ROM, mask-set chip selects

## Function

A memory of 4096 bytes, fixed when the chip is made. The byte at the address on `A0`–`A11` appears on `O0`–`O7` about 450 ns after the address settles, while both chip selects are active. Otherwise the outputs are off (floating), so that several memories can share the same data lines.

Whether each chip select is active high or active low is also chosen when the chip is made. The Part's `select=` says it: one letter for each of `CS1` and `CS2`, `L` for active low and `H` for active high. That is why the pin names carry no `_N`. On the ZX80, where the parts list calls it an 8332, both are active low (`select=LL`): `CS1` on the ROM select, `CS2` on `A12`, so the ROM fills only the lower 4K of each 8K.

The outputs come on about 120 ns after the selects become active, and go off about 100 ns after one becomes inactive. These are the data sheet's maximums; no typical values are published.

Its contents come from the file that `contents=` names: a `.rom` text file (with `offset=` for a slice of a larger one) or a raw 4096-byte `.bin` dump.

## Pins

- `A0`–`A7`, `A8`, `A9`, `A10`, `A11`: address; `A0` is the lowest bit
- `O0`–`O7`: data out; `O0` is the lowest bit
- `CS1`, `CS2`: chip selects, each active high or low as the chip was made
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
