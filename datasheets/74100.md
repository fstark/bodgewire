# 74100 — 8-bit bistable latch

## Function

Eight latches in two groups of four, each group with its own enable. While an enable (`1G` or `2G`) is high, its four outputs follow their data inputs. When it goes low, they keep the value the inputs had just before, until it goes high again.

On the PET 2001 it remembers where each line of characters starts in screen memory, so the address counters can go back to it for each of the line's eight rows of dots.

## Pins

- `1D1`–`1D4`: data inputs of group 1
- `1Q1`–`1Q4`: outputs of group 1
- `1G`: enable of group 1, high to follow, low to hold
- `2D1`–`2D4`: data inputs of group 2
- `2Q1`–`2Q4`: outputs of group 2
- `2G`: enable of group 2
- `NC1`, `NC6`, `NC13`, `NC14`: not connected inside the chip
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
