# KEY — key switch

## Function

A keyboard key switch (Cherry MX footprint) under a plain keycap with its legend. Its two contacts are joined while the key is held down. In a key matrix one contact goes to a row and the other to a column: while the key is down, the scanned row reaches its column. Three keys held at three corners of a rectangle join the fourth corner's row and column too (a ghost key). Pressing it on a board in the game: hold the left mouse button on the cap; right-click keeps it down until the next click; while the Machine has the keys (Typing: a click on the keypad, the shelf's keyboard or F2), hold its key on the PC keyboard.

## Pins

- `1`, `2`: the two contacts; either way round
