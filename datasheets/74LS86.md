# 74LS86 — quad 2-input XOR

## Function

Four separate exclusive-OR gates. Each output is high when its two inputs differ, and low when they are the same. With one input tied high, a gate works as an inverter for the other.

- A low, B low: Y low
- A low, B high: Y high
- A high, B low: Y high
- A high, B high: Y low

## Pins

- `1A`–`4A`, `1B`–`4B`: inputs; gate n uses nA and nB
- `1Y`–`4Y`: outputs
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
