# 74LS08 — quad 2-input AND

## Function

Four separate AND gates. Each output is high only when both of its inputs are high; otherwise it is low.

- A low, B low: Y low
- A low, B high: Y low
- A high, B low: Y low
- A high, B high: Y high

## Pins

- `1A`–`4A`, `1B`–`4B`: inputs; gate n uses nA and nB
- `1Y`–`4Y`: outputs
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
