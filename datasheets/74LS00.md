# 74LS00 — quad 2-input NAND

## Function

Four separate NAND gates. Each output is low only when both of its inputs are high; otherwise it is high.

- A low, B low: Y high
- A low, B high: Y high
- A high, B low: Y high
- A high, B high: Y low

## Pins

- `1A`–`4A`, `1B`–`4B`: inputs; gate n uses nA and nB
- `1Y`–`4Y`: outputs
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
