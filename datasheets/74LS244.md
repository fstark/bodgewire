# 74LS244 — octal buffer, tri-state

## Function

Eight one-way buffers in two groups of four. While a group's `OE_N` is low, each of its `Y` outputs copies its `A` input. While `OE_N` is high, the group's outputs are off (floating), so that other chips can drive the same lines: a bus driver. Tie `OE_N` low for buffers that are always on.

## Pins

- `1A1`–`1A4`: inputs of the first group
- `1Y1`–`1Y4`: outputs of the first group
- `1OE_N`: output enable of the first group, active low
- `2A1`–`2A4`: inputs of the second group
- `2Y1`–`2Y4`: outputs of the second group
- `2OE_N`: output enable of the second group, active low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
