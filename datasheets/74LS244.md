# 74LS244 — octal buffer, tri-state

## Function

Eight one-way buffers in two groups of four. While `1OE_N` is low, `1Y1`–`1Y4` copy `1A1`–`1A4`; while it is high, they are off (floating), so that other chips can drive the same lines: a bus driver. `2OE_N` does the same for `2A1`–`2A4` and `2Y1`–`2Y4`. Tie an enable low for buffers that are always on.

## Pins

- `1A1`–`1A4`: inputs of the first group
- `1Y1`–`1Y4`: outputs of the first group
- `1OE_N`: output enable of the first group, active low
- `2A1`–`2A4`: inputs of the second group
- `2Y1`–`2Y4`: outputs of the second group
- `2OE_N`: output enable of the second group, active low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
