# 74LS373 — octal transparent latch, tri-state

## Function

Eight latches with one shared enable `LE`. While `LE` is high, each `Q` output follows its `D` input (the latch is transparent). When `LE` goes low, each latch keeps the value its `D` had just before, and ignores `D` until `LE` goes high again.

While `OE_N` is low the outputs show the latches; while it is high they are off (floating), and the latches keep working. Tie `OE_N` low for outputs that are always on.

On the ZX80 it holds the character code the RAM gave during a display fetch: its low six bits address the character in the ROM, bit 6 decides whether a NOP is forced, bit 7 inverts the character.

## Pins

- `1D`–`8D`: data inputs
- `1Q`–`8Q`: outputs
- `LE`: latch enable, active high (transparent while high)
- `OE_N`: output enable, active low
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
