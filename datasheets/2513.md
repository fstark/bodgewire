# 2513 — 64-character 5 x 7 character generator ROM

## Function

A memory holding the dots of 64 characters, each 8 lines of 5 dots. `A4`–`A9` pick the character: the low 6 bits of its ASCII code (upper case letters, digits and punctuation). `A1`–`A3` pick the line of the character, 0 at the top; line 0 is blank in every character, the 5 × 7 shape is on lines 1–7. `O5`–`O1` are that line's dots, `O5` the leftmost, high for a lit dot.

The dots come out about 450 ns after a line change and 500 ns after a character change, while `CE_N` is low; with `CE_N` high the outputs are off (floating).

The real chip also needs −5 V (`VDD`) and −12 V (`VGG`). Those supplies are not part of these Boards: the two pins are left unconnected.

Its contents come from the file that `contents=` names. With `offset=` (a multiple of the chip's size, `$hex` or decimal), the chip holds only the slice of a larger `.rom` text file that starts there: one program can fill several sockets, as the BW-2001's BASIC fills `$C000` and `$C800`. Words the file never wrote are erased (`$FF`). A raw `.bin` dump is one chip and takes no offset.

## Pins

- `A1`–`A3`: line within the character, `A1` the lowest bit
- `A4`–`A9`: character, `A4` the lowest bit
- `O1`–`O5`: dots of the line, `O5` leftmost, `O1` rightmost
- `CE_N`: chip enable, active low
- `VGG`, `VDD`: negative supplies of the real chip, not connected
- `NC2`, `NC3`, `NC9`, `NC13`, `NC23`: not connected
- `VCC`: +5 V
- `GND`: 0 V
