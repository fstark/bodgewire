# BUTTON — push button

## Function

Two contacts joined while the button is held down, apart when it is released. Here one contact goes to 0 V (or +5 V) and the other to a signal with a pull-up (or pull-down) resistor: the signal sits at the resistor's level while released and reads the rail while pressed. Pressing it on a board in the game: hold the left mouse button on its body; right-click keeps it pressed until the next click.

## Pins

- `1`, `2`: the two contacts; either way round
