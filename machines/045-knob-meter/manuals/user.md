# Knob Meter

## What it does

Connect +5 V to `J1` and plug the paddle controller into `J2`. The two digits `DS1` and `DS2` show how far the controller's knob is turned: 00 at the left stop, 99 at the right stop, about the knob's percentage between. The reading is renewed ten times a second, so it follows the knob as it turns. At power-on the digits may show any number or odd shapes for a moment.
