# Stopwatch

## What it does

Connect +5 V to `J1`. Press `START` (`SW1`) to start and `STOP` (`SW2`) to stop; `RESET` (`SW3`) resets the count to 0.00. The three digits `DS1`, `DS2` and `DS3` read seconds, tenths and hundredths, counting up to 9.99 and then on from 0.00. Started again after `STOP`, it continues from the same count. At power-on the display may show any number or odd shapes, and may already be counting: press `STOP`, then `RESET`.
