# Stopwatch

## What it does

Connect +5 V to `J1`. Press `SW1` to start and `SW2` to stop; `SW3` resets the count to 0.00. The three digits `DS1`, `DS2` and `DS3` read seconds, tenths and hundredths, counting up to 9.99 and then on from 0.00. Stop continues from the same count when started again. At power-on the display may show any number or odd shapes, and may already be counting: press Stop, then Reset.
