# Recipe — Add an instrument

A measuring tool placed on the Board, of two shapes: Equipment with a window (logic analyzer P3c, scope P3d), or held with no window, its readout drawn by the Shelf (logic probe P2g; multimeter P3d, held since P8g: `BoardView::meterPress()` gives its press, `ui/meter_lcd` draws its LCD over the Shelf; it is no `BenchObject` and `IsInstrument` is false, `Measures` true). A held one skips the Window row below. Threads: sim → devices (Consumers) → UI ([ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md)).

Reference commits (P3d, multimeter + scope): `ecb3915` placement + volts, `53327ba`/`1414e2b` engines, `7180f0c` consumers, `2bdcbe2` Session, `6c06d28` tool/window pairing, `94dd63b`/`d3388bf` windows. P5e (an instrument widened): `168402f` analyzer engine (32 Channels, mask Triggers, bus patterns), `ceaf4a0` scope delay, watch budget and Dispatcher merge, `7c45e1b` Channel Groups in the Session, `ddd0f7a` windows. P5f (windows made coherent): `d3759d1` time view maths, `2bf1bb3` analyzer window and flags, `f24ff06` scope window.

## Layers, in phase order

| Layer | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | `src/instruments/<x>.{hpp,cpp}` (bw_instruments) | pure state fed with `Sample`s and `advanceTo(Time)`; no threads, no sim; reading struct for display. Unit tests `tests/instruments/<x>_test.cpp` |
| Headless check | `tests/machines/<feature>_test.cpp` | engine on a real Machine via `Simulator` + `Recorder` (cf. `scope_meter_test.cpp`) |
| Consumer | `src/runner/<x>_consumer.{hpp,cpp}` (bw_runner) | implements `Consumer` (`src/runner/consumer.hpp`): `nets()`, `powerOn`, `sample`, `seed`, `advanceTo`, `powerOff`; retargeting via `touch(runner, net)` + counted watch (an instrument whose tip touches one Net derives from `SingleNetConsumer<State>`, `src/runner/single_net_consumer.hpp`, as the probe and the meter do, and keeps only its publishing); UI → devices config through an atomic generation; devices → UI through atomics/`TripleBuffer`. Tests `tests/runner/<x>_consumer_test.cpp` |
| Session | `src/session/<x>_setup.{hpp,cpp}`, `src/session/session.{hpp,cpp}` | a setup class (as `ProbeSetup`, `MeterSetup`, `ScopeSetup`, `AnalyzerSetup`) owns the placement (tip / lead / clip / hook: Layer + Point) and settings across rebuilds; `bind(consumer)` at each build finds the Nets (`build_->machine->probeNet(...)`, `PinNet`) and configures the consumer; it is private, with the Session a friend. The Session adds the consumer to `Live` and `makeLive()`, the setup's `bind` to `bindBuild()` and an accessor (`session.scope()`). Tests in `tests/session/session_test.cpp` |
| Placement rule | `src/view/board_scene.{hpp,cpp}` | `AcceptsLead`/`LeadLabel`: held tips take any copper, attached clips/hooks take pads only; refusal says why |
| Focus | `src/view/focus.{hpp,cpp}`, `src/view/bench_places.hpp`, `src/ui/shelf.cpp`, `src/ui/shelf_drawings.cpp`, `src/ui/tool_click.cpp` | an `Owner`, and the `BenchObject` of its window, which `ObjectOf` gives (`Focus` keeps whether it is out), tested in `tests/view/focus_test.cpp`; an `Item` with its vector drawing (lit when out) on the shelf (no key: P6o), with its tray and hint in `shelf.cpp`'s `kItems` and its `OwnerOf` and `WindowOf` cases; the click case in `ToolClick`'s switch on the owner, with `refuse(reason)` for a click it cannot act on (the shelf and the Board both say it, P6c). | Window | `src/ui/<x>_window.{hpp,cpp}`, `src/ui/CMakeLists.txt`, `src/ui/main.cpp`, `src/view/<x>_layout.{hpp,cpp}` | a drawn case with no title bar (the Oscilloscope since P8h, the Logic Analyzer since P8i: `scope_window.cpp`, `analyzer_window.cpp`), `bool draw(bool& open, Session&)` called from main's `instrument(owner, window)`. Begun as the TV is: `PlaceNext(object, first size)`, the size constraints (its minimal size, clamped to the bench), `ImGui::Begin` under its name constant (`k<X>WindowName`, for the shelf's `BringToFront`/`InFront`) with `NoTitleBar`, `NoScrollbar`, `NoScrollWithMouse`; a `BenchObject` with its place and sizes in `src/view/bench_places.cpp` (tested in `tests/view/bench_places_test.cpp`). Its boxes from a pure layout in `src/view` (tested), built on `view/case_strip` (the strip's sizes, `LayOutStepper`, `LayOutBadge`, `SizeKeyBox`, `BandAt`); drawn with `ui/case_keys` (`DrawCaseFrame`, `DrawGroup`, `DrawGroupLabel`, `DrawBadge`, `DrawBezel`, keys, LEDs, lamps, rockers, `TimeGrid` for an LED's values by unit). Its keys are InvisibleButtons over their boxes, drawn pressed while held; the size key calls `NextInstrumentSize`; `InstrumentClicked(kCaseClose)` returns a click (it then takes the focus); it ends with `CaseClose` (puts it away), `PopOnDoubleClick`, `CornerGrip`, `PinchResize`; `PlaceNext` keeps it on the bench or, popped out, gives it its own desktop window (a double-click on its shelf drawing or its body, `TogglePop`, `--pop`, P6j, P6o); no gold (the gold is on its shelf drawing). ## Gotchas

- `kMaxWatched` = 128 watched Nets for devices + every instrument together. Rings are allocated, not written: an unused slot costs no memory. The Dispatcher merges Samples with a heap over the slots that hold some; never go back to scanning every slot or every Consumer's `nets()` per Sample (a 32-Clip bus is millions of Samples a second).
- A Net watched again gets no new Sample until it changes: implement `Consumer::seed` or it reads X.
- `configure()` while Off must set the UI-side phase directly (no slices arrive).
- `Runner::powerOff` is queued: `waitIdle()` before checking; `waitIdle` hangs if Running without `pauseAt`.
- Short tests: a Capture is 65 536 points: 655 µs at 100 MHz, 6.5 ms at 10 MHz, 65 ms at 1 MHz. Runner and Session tests use 100 MHz.
- Reading a bus in a Capture: take the data at the last point of the cycle with `PHI2` high, not the cycle's last point (the 6502 lets go of the data 40 ns before it changes the address; a floating bus with TTL inputs reads `FF`).
- Threads: run the session/runner tests under TSan (separate build dir with `-fsanitize=thread`).
- Release gate: 1.00× with every instrument attached on the busiest Machine, UI under ~1 ms (`bw_ui --screenshot` prints both).
- Every bench window goes through `PlaceNext` (or `OnBench` for one that never leaves the bench) before its `Begin`: with ImGui's multi-viewports on (P6j), a window left to ImGui becomes a desktop window when dragged past the main window's edge. `TogglePop` takes the window's name (its last place and size); with no desktop windows (the web build, `--one-window`) it fills the bench with the window instead, as the size key does for its third preset.
- `PlaceNext` puts a window at its default place and first size the first time it comes out (`FirstUseEver`), shrunk and moved in on a bench smaller than it (P8f); after that both are the player's for the bench (no way back to the default place: P6o); popped back in, it returns where it was on the bench before (`ReturnPlace`). Several instruments out at normal size overlap: the places are for one at a time.
- A press on a window's close button is not a click on the window: ImGui closes at the release, a frame later, and counting the press would take the focus from the tool that has it (`InstrumentClicked`).
- ImGui default fonts (vector and bitmap) lack `–`/`—`, `−`, `Δ`, `◀`: use `Displayable`, ASCII `-`, `dt`, and draw arrows as triangles. They have `µ`, `·`, `×`.
- The agent can't see the live screen: use `bw_ui … --screenshot X.png --after 1s` (release build) and view the PNG. The analyzer opens at its first view (10 sample points per division about the Trigger: bus values readable at 100 ns); `--view 1us:+5us`, `--marker +2us`, `--cursor +3.7us` set the rest. At `2us` a 1 µs bus cycle is too narrow for its text in the default window.
- Window input: read pan and zoom once per frame before drawing anything that depends on the view (lanes, but also an axis or overview above them: reserve their rows, draw them after). A canvas over time reads its gestures with `TimeCanvas::read` (`src/ui/gestures`), as the scope and the analyzer do, not from ImGui itself: it takes the wheel so zooming never scrolls the window (P3c's intermittent navigation bugs), steps a fractional trackpad wheel on whole notches, and gives a drag from its press (one that rounds, as the scope's delay to tenths of a division, would never move if added up frame by frame). A round knob reads `ReadKnob`. `read(width, divisions, markerX, top, vertical)` (P8f): `markerX` and `top` make a click on the Marker's flag clear it; `vertical` is for a canvas whose rows scroll (a drag then pans or scrolls, by its first move: `scrollY`); `scrub` is a finger's hold, where the cursor goes and stays; a Marker and a cursor land on an edge of their row or band with `SnapToEdge` (`src/view/time_view.hpp`), the mouse's as a finger's (P8h, P8i).
- The window's controls come from `src/ui/controls.hpp` (P8f), the same for the mouse and a finger: `Stepper` for a value out of a list, `Choice` for a few choices (a drawn key's: `ChoicesPopup`), `NumberField` for a number; no `Combo`, slider or `InputText`. Every control is at least `kControl` (28 px) high; a line that holds a `Stepper` has all of its controls `kBig` (44 px) high, its labels drawn by `BigText`. Nothing may need a hover, a right-click or a modifier alone: give it a control, and name the control in the hint.
- Controls over a canvas (the scope's plates over its screen, P8h): `IsItemHovered` tests an item's own rectangle only, so the canvas keeps its hover, wheel and double-click under them unless it allows overlap. Submit the canvas's item first with `ImGui::SetNextItemAllowOverlap()`; every item submitted after it over it (and `CornerGrip`, last) then takes the hover from it. Apply what changes the bands or rows (an unhook, an unclip, a group, a move) after the drawing and after the settings are written back, or the frame draws a gone Hook and its copy of the settings undoes what the unhook changed.
- The window ends with `PopOnDoubleClick`, `CornerGrip` and `PinchResize(taken)`, `taken` while two fingers are the time screen's (they zoom it). Rows that scroll inside a drawn case are scrolled by hand (the analyzer's, P8i: an offset, the canvas's `scrollY`, the wheel over the plates, two fingers), not in a child window: a child would take the corner grip's hover and draw over it, and its scrollbar would narrow the lanes under a full-width axis. Their items go inside `ImGui::PushClipRect` of the rows' box, so a row half scrolled off is not hovered over the axis.
- Draw long Captures from runs (`CaptureRuns`), jumping sub-pixel runs by binary search: per-point loops over 65 536 points × 32 lanes blow the UI budget.
