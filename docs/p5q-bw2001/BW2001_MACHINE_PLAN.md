## Implementation notes

- Before the docs (2026-11-15): the three schematic sheets transcribed by three agents in parallel ([schematic/](../machines/130-bw2001/schematic/README.md)); joined with stand-ins (two 74LS138 halves for the 74LS139, no MC3446s or edge connectors, a gate-made composite sync for today's TV), they boot BASIC 1 to its banner on the TV in the right place, release 0.24× headless. The 8 MHz clock and its gated copy were 28 % of all Net changes, the 1 MHz phase clocks another 15 %.
- Docs review (before the docs commit), fixed: the TV mode enum is `TvSync` (`TvInput` is the headless plug class) and the consumer's `powerOn` keeps the mode; `MONITOR`'s grounds plain `Input`s on `GND` and its unused pin `NoConnect` (a `Power` pin `GND1` breaks the builder), `MATRIXKB`'s too; datasheets for the three passive connectors; the retrace wait covers screen reads as well (`SEL8` has no R/W gating) and its timing budget (about 22 cycles after the `PB5` read); the model's VIA `ORB` reads and `PCR` write not flagged as unmodelled, and it counts screen accesses in the display window; the harness's Machine target holds keys by row and column; `DS1` gets an `edge` rule; stage 1 ties `RW_E` and holds `SEL8` low through `UE5`, and ties the shared chips' sheet-1 inputs; `DriveOf(PullUp, X)` is `Port` and the existing pull-up case is rewritten; our reset writes VIA `PCR` = `$0C` (the first character set, as BASIC 1), else the TV shows the lower-case half; `Ls139` a class as `Ls138`; passive connector and `MATRIXKB` pins `sampled`; the 2 × 12 header's pads keep each PET letter opposite its number; `MatrixKeyFor` (the harness has `Bw2001KeyFor`); `bw_ui --type` accepts a `MATRIXKB`; held matrix keys released at power-off; chained `next` resistors and the `angle` field new; `R27` added, `R42` dropped, sheet 2's `R12` and the `VERT_DRIVE` pull-down left out; the stuck-Fault strength wording; phase 4's fence runs the P5p netlist; debug timings measured in phase 8, long cases release-only, cases named `bw2001 machine: stage N`.
- Phase 1: fence (logic, 6520/6522/6530, bw1 Puzzles, P5n bench, bw2001) green first time: nothing relied on a port's 0 beating an output.
- Phases 3–5 committed together: phase 4 edited the Session, CLI and catalogue that phase 3's fence had just passed. A typed key is drawn down from the Part's own state (`MatrixKeyboard::scripted`, an atomic for the UI thread). PC typing goes through the queue (40 ms down, 40 ms up in simulation time), not held in real time as the design said: at 0.24× a quick tap is about one retrace, which the editor may miss. The keycap font has no arrows: ←, ↑ and the CRSR arrows are drawn.
- Phase 6: `next` beside a PET chip is accepted by the parser once `grid pet` is given; free parts go to the empty cell nearest the parts sharing their Nets (greedy, not annealed); `UDE9` (not a cell) is such a free part.
- Phase 7: `clear()` in the firmware test waits 300 ms of model time after CLR (a clear is now about nine frames). Release: the P5p netlist's whole conformance set green in 3 min 11 s.
- Phase 8: the netlist is generated from the transcribed sheets with the Machine design's changes (the script stayed in the session's scratch; the netlist's header names its sources). `UH2`/`UH6` left out (empty sockets: no chip). The first cases showed every cell at a multiple of 64 (not a row start) drawn as the cell before: the carry through `UD5`, `UD6`, `UD7` (about 100 ns), a 450 ns 2114 and a 450 ns 2316B miss the next character load by about 50 ns. The real board works, so its 2114s are faster than the 450 ns grade: `2114 access=` (450, 300 or 200 ns: the 2114, -3, -2) added, the BW-2001's 2114s at 300 ns (a choice to confirm with the user). The TV needs about 100 ms after power-on to lock (the vertical state starts at random); the cases look at 150 ms. Debug ratio about 0.004× (3 × 150 ms in 72 s). Release: **0.49×** headless (`bw run --time 2s`), **0.51×** in `bw_ui` without a Board. The text sits right of centre on the TV (TV columns 144–463 of 512).
- Phase 9: sheets 1 and 2 joined, our ROMs: the banner and `READY` on the TV at 399 ms (seed 1), first time; no screen access during the display over the boot (1073 accesses, all in the retrace); the stage-1 cases unchanged (at 150 ms the reset code is still sizing the RAM, the screen still the power-on bytes). `UJ9` gates 3–4 tied. Release: **0.24×** headless, **0.23×** in `bw_ui` without a Board. Debug: the four Machine cases in 4 min.
- Phase 10: the edges joined; every case passed first time but one expectation (BASIC 1 leaves a blank row before `READY.`). Release cases: typing through `J5` (`PRINT 2+2`), a 30-line `FOR … PRINT` scrolling ten times without a screen access in the display, the self-test by `DIAG` grounded (the netlist's `DIAG` line rewritten), session `27_cls` on the Machine, and the real BASIC 1 (the empty `UH2`, `UH6` fitted by the test): its banner on the TV, no snow while it prints, then `POKE 59458,62`: over 100 screen accesses in the display while a loop prints (snow), `VIDEO_ON` never X, and a line typed afterwards echoes and runs (the port's `Port` strength: the retrace interrupt goes on). The manuals checker takes one section a manual: the self-test is in "What it does", the service notes went to BW2001.md. Debug fence (`bw2001|manuals`) 44/44 in 147 s.
- Phase 11: headers standing upright reached 30–50 mm into the Board: under `grid pet` a pin header on the back or front edge now lies along it (`54d13c3`), 396 × 300 mm instead of 373 deep. The address pull-ups `R28`–`R38` run as one row under `UE2`–`UE4`, the keyboard's `R18`–`R25` both ways from `UG8`'s middle (chained from one end they left the Board). `bw place`: 51.4 m of wire. Routed by the user (2026-11-15) as placed; `check-board` ok, KiCad DRC 0 unconnected, one silkscreen warning (`R99`'s reference). The user found the chips' orientation on the real 320137 differs (at least): a pass bringing the Board closer to the real one comes after P5q.
- Phase 12: the Machine's cases run on the Board's circuit (the netlist keeps "loads without warnings" and the text variants); every case green in debug and release first time. `session: bw2001 with every instrument` (32 Clips on the 6502, 4 Hooks, probe, meter; the TV locked at 150 ms). Release `bw_ui netlists/bw2001.bwn` on the routed Board with every instrument: **0.22×**, UI **2.6 ms** (2.3 ms without instruments: the 400-Net Board's traces; over the ~1 ms of the recipe, left for the speed row). Screenshots viewed: the banner, `PRINT 2+2` typed through `J5`, the keyboard window, the instruments. BASIC 1 (a scratch netlist with the user's ROMs, no Board): without the poke a `PRINT CHR$(19);I,I,I,I` loop prints at the top with a clean picture; with `POKE 59458,62` it runs several times faster and specks of snow show beside the text. Scrolling blanks whole frames under BASIC 1 (its editor holds `BLANK_TV` low while it scrolls, as the PET 2001's did): a screenshot can land on a black frame.
- Phase 13: recipes (ADD_PART: the `Port` strength, transceiver pins, speed grades, the Motorola data book; ADD_CONNECTOR_DEVICE: passive connectors, monitors, key matrices, typing into a slow Machine, drawn arrows; ADD_MACHINE: a real board from its schematic, stages of a real board, critical paths and speed grades, watching a rare event, `grid pet`), GLOSSARY (Snow), GAME_DESIGN ladder row 130, ARCHITECTURE: the P5q row done, P5q1 (the Board as the 320137, the user's suggestion) and P5q2 (a kernel for the BW-2001).
- User check pending: `bw_ui netlists/bw2001.bwn`: the banner; type in the keyboard window (click it or F2), PC keys by character, paste a program (Cmd+V); `10 FOR I=1 TO 30:PRINT I:NEXT`, `RUN` (scrolls a row at a time, no snow); `PRINT USR(61440)`, `.M 0400 0420`, `.X`; the self-test by a Bodge Wire from `J2` pad 9 (`DIAG`) to `J2` pad 1, then power off and on; the instruments on `PHI0`, `HORZ_DRIVE`, `SEL8`. BASIC 1 and the killer poke need a local netlist with the ROMs (the Machine tests build one).
- Full debug suite: 1196 of 1197 in 688 s, the release-only killer poke case skipped as designed; the one failure was `simulator: a strong 0 beats a pull-up pin's 1; its own 0 is strong`, a case phase 1's fence missed (its name matched no filter): it asserted the old rule (a port's 0 against an output's 1 reads X). Renamed and changed to the new rule (1); `simulator|logic|resolve` 54/54. The release-only Machine cases passed in phases 10 and 12.
